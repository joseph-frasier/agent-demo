Wilderness Education for Every Journey

Ridgeline Outdoor Education offers comprehensive programs designed to meet you where you are and take you further. Our courses combine expert instruction, hands-on practice, and the transformative power of Northern Arizona's wilderness to build competence, confidence, and community.

01 / WILDERNESS SKILLS

Wilderness Survival Courses

Learn to thrive in backcountry environments through comprehensive survival training that covers the essential skills every wilderness traveler should possess. Our courses teach shelter construction using natural materials, fire making in various conditions, water sourcing and purification, navigation with map, compass and natural indicators, wild plant identification and foraging, and emergency preparedness protocols.

You'll practice these skills under the guidance of experienced wilderness educators who emphasize understanding principles over memorizing techniques. Whether you're preparing for extended backcountry trips or simply want the confidence that comes from knowing you could handle yourself in remote settings, these courses provide thorough, practical training grounded in respect for nature's power and generosity.

What You'll Learn

  • Shelter building using natural materials
  • Fire making techniques for various conditions
  • Navigation with map, compass, and natural indicators
  • Wild plant identification and foraging
  • Emergency preparedness protocols

03 / EXPEDITIONS

Guided Backpacking Expeditions

Experience Northern Arizona's stunning wilderness landscapes on expert-led backpacking trips designed for various skill levels and aspirations. Our guides handle route planning, permitting, and safety oversight while teaching Leave No Trace principles and backcountry techniques throughout the journey.

Expeditions range from accessible overnight trips perfect for those new to backpacking, to challenging multi-day treks through rugged terrain for experienced hikers seeking deeper wilderness immersion. Along the way, you'll learn efficient packing strategies, campsite selection, navigation skills, wilderness cooking, and the rhythms of backcountry travel. These aren't guided tours—they're educational experiences where you develop real competence while exploring some of Arizona's most beautiful wild places in the supportive company of skilled instructors and fellow adventurers.

Trip Options

  • Beginner-friendly overnight trips
  • Multi-day wilderness treks
  • Leave No Trace education
  • Skill instruction throughout journey

05 / CERTIFICATION

Wilderness First Aid Certification

Prepare to manage medical emergencies in remote settings where professional help is hours away through our Wilderness First Aid and First Responder certification courses. Our curriculum combines classroom instruction covering wilderness-specific medical protocols with extensive hands-on scenario practice that simulates the challenges of backcountry emergency response.

You'll learn patient assessment systems, injury stabilization techniques, environmental emergency management, evacuation decision-making, and communication strategies for remote incidents. Our instructors bring real experience from wilderness settings and create realistic training scenarios that prepare you for the stress and complexity of actual emergencies. These certifications meet industry standards required for outdoor professionals while providing recreational backcountry travelers with skills and confidence that could save lives when trails end and help is distant.

Certification Courses

  • Wilderness First Aid (WFA)
  • Wilderness First Responder (WFR)
  • Scenario-based training
  • Industry-standard certification
