Program Registration

Ready to register for an upcoming wilderness course, youth camp, or guided expedition? Contact us to check availability for your preferred dates and receive registration materials. We'll provide detailed information about what to expect, what to bring, and how to prepare.

For team building retreats and custom programs, we'll schedule a consultation to discuss your group's specific needs, objectives, and logistics before designing a tailored experience.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions we receive include inquiries about:

  • Experience prerequisites for different courses
  • Physical fitness requirements
  • What gear is provided vs. what to bring
  • Scholarship availability and group discounts

We're happy to address these and any other questions. No question is too basic—we'd rather you ask before arriving than feel uncertain.
